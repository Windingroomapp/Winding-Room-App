Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Winding Room respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Winding Room mobile application.

Information You Add to Winding Room

Winding Room allows you to store information about your watch collection, including watch details, photographs, ownership information, wear history, service history, financial information, documents, notes, watch-health results, and other collection-related information you choose to enter.

Winding Room is designed as a local-first application. Your collection information is primarily stored on your device and is not sent to the Winding Room developer by default.

iCloud Backup and Restore

If you choose to use Winding Room's iCloud backup or restore features, information you elect to back up may be stored through Apple services associated with your Apple Account. Apple is responsible for its own handling of iCloud data under Apple's privacy policies and terms.

Winding Room does not operate a separate cloud account system for your collection.

Photos and Camera

Winding Room may request access to your camera or photo library when you choose to photograph, import, or save watch-related images. These permissions are used only to provide features you request.

Microphone

Winding Room may request microphone access for Watch Health Check and acoustic watch-analysis features. Microphone input is used to perform the requested analysis. Winding Room is not designed to use microphone access for advertising, tracking, or unrelated monitoring.

Face ID and Device Authentication

Winding Room may use Face ID or the device authentication system to protect access to certain sensitive information or features. Biometric information is handled by iOS and is not provided to or stored by Winding Room.

Siri and Apple Services

If you choose to use Winding Room features through Siri, App Intents, Shortcuts, iCloud, or other Apple platform services, information necessary to perform the request may be processed by Apple according to Apple's privacy policies and your device settings.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Winding Room may offer Premium features through an auto-renewable subscription processed by Apple through the App Store. Winding Room does not receive or store your full payment-card information. Apple manages payment processing, billing, renewal, cancellation, and related transaction information. The app may receive purchase or entitlement status necessary to provide Premium features.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Winding Room does not currently include third-party advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs and does not use your data for cross-app or cross-website advertising tracking.

Apple may independently provide App Store, diagnostic, crash, or platform information under Apple's own policies and your device or account settings.

Data Sharing

Winding Room does not sell your personal information. Information may leave your device when you intentionally use features such as:

  • iCloud backup or restore;
  • exporting a report, PDF, image, backup, or other document;
  • sharing content through Apple's system sharing features; or
  • using an Apple platform service that you choose to invoke.

Those actions occur at your direction.

Data Retention and Deletion

Collection data generally remains on your device until you edit or delete it, reset the relevant app data, or remove the application and its associated local data. Information stored in an iCloud backup may remain available according to Apple's iCloud storage and backup behavior until you remove or replace that backup.

Children's Privacy

Winding Room is not specifically directed toward children under 13 and is not designed as a service for collecting personal information from children.

Security

Winding Room uses security capabilities provided by iOS and Apple platforms where appropriate. No software or storage system can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their devices and Apple Accounts with appropriate security settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Winding Room, applicable law, or platform requirements. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.
